# Lesson, Course, Leo, and Monthly League polish

## What will change
- Replace the long lesson-goal list with three short, action-led outcomes, tighten the lesson entry layout, and give the Start Mission button a restrained lavender glow.
- Compress each Course section banner to roughly 68% of its current height and refine the daily lesson hierarchy to a small day label, 27–30pt title, and industry/duration metadata.
- Turn the existing Course ring into a visible learning orbit without moving or resizing its five activity buttons: solid lavender track, small progress markers, and completed progress accents behind Leo.
- Repair Tap Leo so the conversation scrolls and expands above its controls, the latest reply never covers the talk button, and the keyboard has an obvious dismiss action.
- Convert social competition language and scoring to one calendar-month season: remove “This week” and “All time,” show monthly XP, and update countdown/reset copy.
- Make every league tier selectable so learners can inspect who is in Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Diamond, while keeping their own league highlighted.
- Upgrade the Friends & Rivals and Monthly League imagery using sharper, purpose-built MarketLingo artwork.

## Technical details
- Preserve the existing orbit node geometry exactly; only the track, markers, completion treatment, banner density, and nearby type are changed.
- Use the learner’s local calendar month for visible season boundaries and monthly XP aggregation.
- Keep existing voice recording, transcription, text messaging, audio, haptics, friend actions, and league promotion data intact.
- Validate changed mobile files, check the app error stream, and update the release roadmap.
