# fintech: hand-written lessons, digest for Deep Case authors

## Day 1: What happens when you pay someone
- OPEN: Venmo shows your payment instantly. Moving that money to your bank can take one to three business days. | Inside Venmo, a payment updates both balances right away, because it is an entry in Venmo's own records. Getting that money into a bank account is a different trip. Venmo's free standard transfer typically arrives within 1 to 3 business days, and its paid instant transfer typically arrives within 30 minutes. The gap exists because money between banks moves in stages. Someone sends an instruction, the system works out who owes what, and only then do banks move real money between them. This lesson maps those stages and the institutions behind them.  [src: https://help.venmo.com/hc/en-us/articles/235399967-Standard-Bank-Transfers-FAQ]
- CARD fed: The Federal Reserve is the bank for banks. Banks keep accounts at one of 12 Reserve Banks.  [src: https://www.federalreserve.gov/aboutthefed/structure-federal-reserve-system.htm https://www.frbservices.org/financial-services/wires https://perkinscoie.com/insights/update/federal-reserve-issues-final-guidelines-master-account-access]
- CARD banks: About 4,300 FDIC-insured banks hold the public's deposits and make loans.  [src: https://www.fdic.gov/quarterly-banking-profile/statistics-glance-industry-trends-fourth-quarter-2025.pdf]
- CARD rails: Clearing comes first, then settlement Clearing matches payment instructions and works out who owes whom. Settlement is the final transfer of money between banks.  [src: https://www.moderntreasury.com/journal/difference-between-settlement-and-clearing https://www.frbservices.org/financial-services/ach https://www.frbservices.org/financial-services/wires]
- CHECK buildChain: Put a bank payment in order. => You send a payment instruction > Clearing works out who owes what > Banks settle at the Fed > The money is final at the other bank
- CHECK multipleChoice: Your Venmo balance updated instantly. What has not happened yet? => Settlement between the banks
- CARD lens-curiosity: Your payment makes two trips First a message travels, then the money follows. The second trip is why bank transfers can take days.  [src: https://help.venmo.com/hc/en-us/articles/235399967-Standard-Bank-Transfers-FAQ]
- CARD rule: A payment is only a message until it settles.  [src: https://www.moderntreasury.com/journal/difference-between-settlement-and-clearing]
- LENS career: Say cleared and settled, not went through People in payments notice precise words. Say a payment cleared through ACH and settled at the Fed.
- LENS invest: Know which layer a company sits on A payments app, a bank and the Fed earn in very different ways. Before judging a fintech, find out which bank it relies on.
- LENS build_startup: Every money app sits on bank rails Most fintech apps do not settle money themselves. A partner bank with a Fed account does it for them.  [src: https://perkinscoie.com/insights/update/federal-reserve-issues-final-guidelines-master-account-access]

## Day 2: How a bank makes money
- OPEN: In October 2026, the average US savings account paid 0.38% a year. The average 30-year mortgage cost 7.40%. | The FDIC's national average savings rate was 0.38% in October 2026, and Freddie Mac's weekly survey put the average 30-year fixed mortgage at 7.40% in mid-October. A bank takes in deposits at one rate and lends or invests them at another. The difference, earned across trillions of dollars, is net interest income. Not every deposit pays as little as a savings account, and not every loan is a mortgage. The logic still holds across the whole balance sheet. In 2025, FDIC-insured banks earned $295.6 billion in net income.  [src: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SNDR https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2026/fdic-insured-institutions-reported-return-assets-124-percent-and-net]
- CARD nim: Net interest margin: interest earned minus interest paid, as a share of earning assets.  [src: https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2026/fdic-insured-institutions-reported-return-assets-124-percent-and-net]
- CARD iorb: Banks also earn interest on the reserves they keep at the Fed.  [src: https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/reserve-balances.htm]
- CARD fees: Fees are the other way banks earn Card fees, account fees and wealth management bring in noninterest income. It does not depend on the rate gap.  [src: https://www.fdic.gov/quarterly-banking-profile/statistics-glance-industry-trends-fourth-quarter-2025.pdf]
- CHECK sortSignal: Sort each source of bank income. => Loan interest -> Interest income; Bond interest -> Interest income; Interest from the Fed -> Interest income; Card fees -> Fee income; Account fees -> Fee income; Wealth management -> Fee income
- CHECK numberSense: A bank's net interest margin is 3.39%. About how many dollars does it net per $1,000 of earning assets a year? => 34dollars
- CARD lens-curiosity: Your savings are the bank's raw material The money in your savings account is what the bank lends to someone else. The rate gap is its pay for doing that.  [src: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/SNDR https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms]
- CARD rule: A bank earns the gap between what money costs it and what it charges for it.  [src: https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2026/fdic-insured-institutions-reported-return-assets-124-percent-and-net]
- LENS career: Know the bank's margin before you walk in Look up the bank's net interest margin and compare it with the industry's 3.39%. It shows you read the numbers.  [src: https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2026/fdic-insured-institutions-reported-return-assets-124-percent-and-net]
- LENS invest: Watch how fast each side reprices When rates move, what a bank pays on deposits and earns on loans rarely move at the same speed. That lag drives the margin.
- LENS build_startup: Look for the fee people dislike Many fintechs grew by cutting a fee banks rely on, like account or transfer fees. Notice which fee a new product would replace.

## Day 3: Who gets a key to the Fed

## Day 5: Why a bank can fail in a day

Regulators closed SVB on March 10. ## Day 6: The bank that closed mid-trade
- OPEN: On June 26, 1974, German authorities closed Bankhaus Herstatt at 3:30 in the afternoon. | At 15:30 Central European Time on June 26, 1974, German authorities closed Bankhaus Herstatt. Some counterparties had irrevocably paid it large amounts of Deutsche marks in currency trades but had not yet received the dollars in exchange, because US markets had only just opened. The danger became known as Herstatt risk, or foreign exchange settlement risk. In September 2002, CLS Bank began settling both legs of currency trades at the same time.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
- CARD gap: The marks were paid in Europe. The dollars were due once US markets opened.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
- CARD name: That danger is now called Herstatt risk, or foreign exchange settlement risk.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
- CARD fix: Pay both sides at once In September 2002, CLS Bank began settling both sides of currency trades together, called payment versus payment.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
- CHECK buildChain: Follow how a Herstatt loss happens. => A bank pays marks to Herstatt in Europe > It waits for dollars due in New York > Regulators close Herstatt that afternoon > The dollars never arrive
- CHECK multipleChoice: How does CLS Bank remove this risk? => It settles both sides of a trade at once
- CHECK theCall: How do you protect yourself? => Settle through payment versus payment
- CARD lens-curiosity: A bank closed mid-afternoon When Herstatt closed in 1974, banks that had paid it marks never received their dollars.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
- CARD rule: In banking, the gap between paying and getting paid is where losses hide.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
- LENS career: Have a view on timing risk Explain the Herstatt case in one line: paid one side, never received the other. It shows you understand settlement.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
- LENS invest: Ask how a firm settles A firm that pays before it receives carries settlement risk. Ask whether it settles both sides together.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
- LENS build_startup: Look for timing gaps Wherever money moves in two steps across time zones, someone carries risk. Ask who, and test whether they would pay to remove it.  [src: https://www.bis.org/publ/qtrpdf/r_qt0212f.pdf]
