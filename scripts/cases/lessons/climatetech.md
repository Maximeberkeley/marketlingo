# climatetech: hand-written lessons, digest for Deep Case authors

## Day 1: The climate science business needs
- OPEN: 2024 was about 1.55°C warmer than the late 1800s. | The World Meteorological Organization combined six datasets and put 2024's global average temperature at 1.55°C above the 1850 to 1900 average, give or take 0.13°C. The ten warmest years on record were 2015 to 2024. WMO stresses that one year above 1.5°C does not mean the Paris goal is lost, since that goal is measured over decades.  [src: https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2024-warmest-year-record-about-155degc-above-pre-industrial-level]
- CARD cause: Burning coal, oil and gas adds CO2, which strengthens the greenhouse effect and traps heat.  [src: https://science.nasa.gov/climate-change/ https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- CARD ppm: CO2 in the air reached 425.7 parts per million in 2025, 52% above pre-industrial levels.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/ https://today.ucsd.edu/story/annual-carbon-dioxide-peak-reaches-432-parts-per-million]
- CARD bench: 1.5°C is measured over decades The Paris goal tracks long-term averages, so one hot year does not breach it. But 2015 to 2024 were the ten warmest years on record.  [src: https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2024-warmest-year-record-about-155degc-above-pre-industrial-level]
- CHECK numberSense: How much warmer than the late 1800s was 2024, in degrees Celsius? => 1.55°C
- CHECK multipleChoice: Does one year above 1.5°C mean the Paris goal is broken? => No, it is measured over decades
- CARD lens-curiosity: The air itself has changed The air now holds 52% more CO2 than it did before the industrial era.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- CARD rule: In climate, emissions drive CO2 levels, and CO2 levels drive warming.  [src: https://science.nasa.gov/climate-change/]
- LENS career: Get the basics exactly right Say 2024 was about 1.55°C above pre-industrial, and that the Paris goal is measured over decades. Precision builds trust.  [src: https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2024-warmest-year-record-about-155degc-above-pre-industrial-level]
- LENS invest: The heat is a business variable The ten warmest years on record were 2015 to 2024. Rising heat is now part of how assets and supply chains are judged.  [src: https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2024-warmest-year-record-about-155degc-above-pre-industrial-level]
- LENS build_startup: Emissions are the root cause Fossil CO2 hit a record 38.1 billion tonnes in 2025. Anything that cuts those emissions works on the cause.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]

## Day 2: Where the money in climate goes

Projected 2025 changes: China up 0.4%, the US up 1.9%, India up 1.4% and the EU up 0.4%. China's emissions growth has slowed considerably.  [src: https://www.cger.nies.go.jp/gcp/pdf/20251113/CarbonBudget2025_CICERO_press_E.pdf https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]

## Day 4: The numbers that frame climate risk
- OPEN: About 170 billion tonnes of CO2 remain in the budget for limiting warming to 1.5°C. | The Global Carbon Budget 2025 estimates the remaining carbon budget for 1.5°C at 170 billion tonnes of CO2, which it calls virtually exhausted: about four years at 2025 emission levels. Fossil emissions reached a record 38.1 billion tonnes, and land-use emissions were projected at 4.1 billion tonnes.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- CARD pace: Fossil CO2 emissions hit a record 38.1 billion tonnes in 2025, up 1.1%.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- CARD ppm: CO2 rose 1.8 ppm in a year, to a May peak of 432 ppm in 2026.  [src: https://today.ucsd.edu/story/annual-carbon-dioxide-peak-reaches-432-parts-per-million]
- CARD budget: A carbon budget is a running total Each year's emissions use up part of the remaining budget. The Global Carbon Project calls the 1.5°C budget virtually exhausted.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- CHECK numberSense: About 170 billion tonnes left, about 42 billion emitted per year. Roughly how many years remain? => 4years
- CHECK multipleChoice: How much fossil CO2 did the world emit in 2025? => 38.1 billion tonnes
- CARD lens-curiosity: About four years At 2025's pace, the world would use up the 1.5°C carbon budget in about four years.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- CARD rule: The budget is fixed: every tonne emitted now leaves less for later.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- LENS career: Quote the budget About 170 billion tonnes left for 1.5°C, around four years at today's pace. That framing shows you know the stakes.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- LENS invest: A shrinking budget raises policy risk Emissions hit a record in 2025 while the 1.5°C budget nears exhaustion. That gap raises the odds of tougher rules.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]
- LENS build_startup: Speed matters With roughly four years of 1.5°C budget left at the current pace, how fast a solution scales matters as much as how well it works.  [src: https://globalcarbonbudget.org/fossil-fuel-co2-emissions-hit-record-high-in-2025/]

## Day 5: The rule that prices carbon
- OPEN: In the EU, a power plant needs a permit for every tonne of CO2 it emits. | The EU Emissions Trading System is a cap-and-trade system. A cap limits total emissions from covered installations, and companies trade allowances, each covering one tonne of CO2 equivalent. Every year, installations must surrender allowances equal to their emissions or pay a fine. It covers power, heavy industry, aviation and maritime, about 36% of EU emissions.  [src: https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2024/02/29/faq-what-is-the-eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/]
- CARD cap: The EU system caps total emissions, and companies trade allowances under the cap.  [src: https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2024/02/29/faq-what-is-the-eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/]
- CARD shrink: The cap shrinks 4.3% a year from 2024 to 2027, then 4.4% a year to 2030.  [src: https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2024/02/29/faq-what-is-the-eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/]
- CARD result: It covers power, industry, aviation and shipping The system covers about 36% of EU emissions. Covered power and industry emissions are 37.3% below 2005.  [src: https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2024/02/29/faq-what-is-the-eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/]
- CARD scopes: Companies count emissions in three scopes: their own, their purchased energy, and their value chain.  [src: https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/home/publications/deloitte/additional-deloitte-guidance/greenhouse-gas-protocol-reporting-considerations/chapter-3-setting-operational-boundaries/3-2-overview-direct-indirect-emissions]
- CHECK spotFake: One of these is false. Tap it. => fake: EU power plants can emit freely without allowances.
- CHECK theCall: What is your long-term plan? => Invest to cut emissions per tonne of steel
- CARD lens-curiosity: Fewer permits every year Each year the EU issues fewer emission permits, aiming for 62% below 2005 levels by 2030.  [src: https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2024/02/29/faq-what-is-the-eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/]
- CARD rule: When carbon has a price, cutting emissions becomes a business decision.  [src: https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2024/02/29/faq-what-is-the-eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/]
- LENS career: Know the scopes Sustainability and finance roles report emissions by scope. Knowing why Scope 3 is hardest to measure is a strong signal.  [src: https://dart.deloitte.com/USDART/home/publications/deloitte/additional-deloitte-guidance/greenhouse-gas-protocol-reporting-considerations/chapter-3-setting-operational-boundaries/3-2-overview-direct-indirect-emissions]
- LENS invest: Carbon cost is real cost EU emitters need an allowance for every tonne, and the cap shrinks each year. Check a company's exposure.  [src: https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2024/02/29/faq-what-is-the-eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/]
- LENS build_startup: A price creates a market Once each tonne costs money, tools that measure or cut emissions save companies real money.  [src: https://carbonmarketwatch.org/2024/02/29/faq-what-is-the-eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets/]
