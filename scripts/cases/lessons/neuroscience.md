## Day 7: The chemicals neurons use to talk
- OPEN: Scientists know of at least 100 chemical messengers in the nervous system. | Neurotransmitters are chemical messengers that carry signals from one nerve cell to the next target cell, whether another neuron, a muscle or a gland. Scientists know of at least 100. Some are excitatory and pass the message on, some are inhibitory and block it, and some are modulatory and adjust how other messengers work. Glutamate is the most common excitatory neurotransmitter and the most abundant in the brain. GABA is the most common inhibitory one. Dopamine plays a role in reward, motivation and learning, and serotonin helps regulate mood, sleep, appetite and pain.  [src: https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/22513-neurotransmitters]
