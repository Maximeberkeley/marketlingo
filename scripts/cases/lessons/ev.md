# ev: hand-written lessons, digest for Deep Case authors

## Day 1: Who makes the world's EVs
- OPEN: In 2025, BYD sold 2.26 million fully electric cars. Tesla delivered 1.64 million. | BYD sold 2,256,714 battery-electric cars in 2025, up 27.9%, plus about 2.29 million plug-in hybrids. Tesla delivered 1,636,129 vehicles. Worldwide, electric car sales grew 20% to more than 20 million, so roughly one in four new cars sold was electric. The EV industry is no longer one company's story. This lesson maps who builds the cars and who builds what is inside them. - CARD players: Old and new companies build EVs Legacy carmakers like Ford are adding EVs, while EV-first companies like Tesla and BYD lead sales. ## Day 2: Where the money in an EV goes
- OPEN: In 2025, Ford's EV unit lost $4.8 billion. | Ford reports three main businesses. In 2025, Model e, its EV unit, posted an EBIT loss of $4.8 billion. Ford Blue, its gas and hybrid vehicles, earned $3.0 billion, and Ford Pro, which serves commercial customers, earned $6.8 billion. Revenue hit a record $187.3 billion, yet Ford reported a net loss of $8.2 billion for the year.  [src: https://s205.q4cdn.com/882619693/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Ford-Q4-2025-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf]
- CARD pack: EV battery packs averaged $99 per kWh in 2025.  [src: https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/]
- CARD china: Packs in China averaged $84 per kWh. North America paid 44% more, Europe 56% more.  [src: https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/]
- CARD credits: Regulatory credits pay EV makers Carmakers that miss emission targets can buy credits from EV makers. Tesla earned about $2 billion this way in 2025.  [src: https://carboncredits.com/tesla-reports-first-ever-annual-revenue-drop-in-2025-carbon-credit-sales-also-dip-28]
- CHECK numberSense: At $99 per kWh, about how much does a 75 kWh battery pack cost? => 7425dollars
- CHECK multipleChoice: Ford's EV unit lost $4.8 billion in 2025. Which business earned the most? => Ford Pro, its commercial vehicles
- CARD lens-curiosity: Same battery, different price A battery pack cost 44% more in North America than in China in 2025, and 56% more in Europe.  [src: https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/]
- CARD rule: In EVs, the cost of the battery decides who can make money.  [src: https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/]
- LENS career: Ask about profit per car Ask how much the company earns or loses on each EV. It is the number every EV team is working to fix.  [src: https://s205.q4cdn.com/882619693/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Ford-Q4-2025-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf]
- LENS invest: Read the segments, not the total Ford's commercial and gas businesses funded a $4.8 billion EV loss. Segment results show where value is made.  [src: https://s205.q4cdn.com/882619693/files/doc_financials/2025/q4/Ford-Q4-2025-Earnings-Press-Release.pdf]
- LENS build_startup: Cost per kWh is the scoreboard EV packs fell to $99 per kWh in 2025. Anything that cuts that number matters to every carmaker. ## Day 3: Who controls the EV supply chain
- OPEN: China made over 80% of the world's battery cells in 2025. | The IEA reports that China accounted for over 80% of battery cell production in 2025. Chinese companies also lead installations: CATL installed 464.7 GWh of EV batteries, 39.2% of the global total of 1,187 GWh, followed by BYD at 16.4%. Battery cost, supply and technology all run through China.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary https://cnevpost.com/2026/02/04/global-ev-battery-market-share-2025/]
- CARD catl: CATL installed 39.2% of all EV batteries in 2025, more than the next two makers combined.  [src: https://cnevpost.com/2026/02/04/global-ev-battery-market-share-2025/]
- CARD tariffs: Tariffs try to shift the balance The US raised its tariff on Chinese EVs to 100% in 2024. The EU added duties of up to 35.3%.  [src: https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/united-states-finalizes-section-301-tariff-increases-imports-china https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/news/eu-commission-imposes-countervailing-duties-imports-battery-electric-vehicles-bevs-china]
- CHECK faceOff: Who has more leverage in a battery supply deal? => CATL
- CHECK theCall: What protects your EV plans? => Add battery suppliers outside China
- CARD lens-curiosity: Built here, powered from there With over 80% of battery cells made in China, many EVs built elsewhere still rely on Chinese suppliers.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- CARD rule: In EVs, power sits with whoever makes the cells.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary https://cnevpost.com/2026/02/04/global-ev-battery-market-share-2025/]
- LENS career: Ask where the cells come from Ask which battery suppliers a carmaker uses and where the cells are made. It shows you understand the real risk.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- LENS invest: Concentration is a moat and a risk CATL's 39.2% share is a moat for CATL and a dependency for every carmaker that relies on it.  [src: https://cnevpost.com/2026/02/04/global-ev-battery-market-share-2025/]
- LENS build_startup: Dependency creates demand for options With over 80% of cells made in China, carmakers elsewhere want other sources. That demand reshapes supply chains.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]

## Day 4: The numbers behind the EV shift
- OPEN: Over 20 million electric cars were sold in 2025, about 1 in 4 new cars. | The IEA's Global EV Outlook 2026 reports that electric car sales grew 20% in 2025 to more than 20 million. It projects 23 million in 2026, or 28% of all car sales.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- CARD regions: The share varies hugely by region Nearly 55% of new cars sold in China in 2025 were electric, and 28% in Europe.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- CARD growth: Global EV sales grew 20% in 2025. Europe's grew more than 30%.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- CHECK numberSense: What share of new cars sold in China in 2025 were electric? => 55%
- CARD price: Average battery pack prices fell 8% in 2025, to $108 per kWh.  [src: https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/]
- CHECK multipleChoice: Which region had the lowest EV share of new car sales in 2025? => The United States
- CARD lens-curiosity: One in four One in four new cars sold worldwide in 2025 was electric. In China, more than half were.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- CARD rule: In EVs, the global average hides huge regional gaps.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- LENS career: Quote the regional split Saying nearly 55% in China, 28% in Europe and under 10% in the US shows you know where the market really is.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- LENS invest: Growth sits in different places Europe's EV sales grew more than 30% in 2025 while the US share held just under 10%. Where a company sells matters.  [src: https://iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary]
- LENS build_startup: Cheaper batteries open new uses With EV packs under $100 per kWh, products that were too costly to electrify a few years ago may now work.  [src: https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/]

## Day 5: The policies that steer EV sales
- OPEN: On September 30, 2025, the US $7,500 tax credit for new EVs ended. | The One Big Beautiful Bill ended the federal clean vehicle credits for purchases after September 30, 2025: $7,500 for new EVs and $4,000 for used ones. Buyers had to sign a contract and make a payment by that date to qualify, which set off a rush to buy before the deadline.  [src: https://blog.turbotax.intuit.com/tax-reform/electric-vehicle-credits-are-ending-soon-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-what-you-need-to-know-125862/]
- CARD rush: Before the deadline, US EV sales hit a record 438,487 in the third quarter of 2025.  [src: https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights-hub/q3-2025-ev-sales-report-commentary/]
- CARD drop: After the credit ended, US EV sales in the first half of 2026 fell nearly 24% from a year earlier.  [src: https://www.moneytalksnews.com/where-the-u-s-ev-market-stands-a-year-after-trump-ended-tax-credits/]
- CARD rules: Tariffs and emission rules steer the market too Tariffs decide which EVs can compete on price. Emission rules create the credits EV makers can sell.  [src: https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/united-states-finalizes-section-301-tariff-increases-imports-china https://carboncredits.com/tesla-reports-first-ever-annual-revenue-drop-in-2025-carbon-credit-sales-also-dip-28]
- CHECK spotFake: One of these is false. Tap it. => fake: US EV sales rose after the credit ended.
- CHECK theCall: How do you plan the next year? => Expect a rush now and a drop after
- CARD lens-curiosity: Why EV sales jumped, then dropped Buyers rushed to claim the $7,500 credit before it ended in September 2025. Then sales fell sharply.  [src: https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights-hub/q3-2025-ev-sales-report-commentary/ https://www.moneytalksnews.com/where-the-u-s-ev-market-stands-a-year-after-trump-ended-tax-credits/]
- CARD rule: In EVs, policy moves demand: when a rule changes, sales follow fast.  [src: https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights-hub/q3-2025-ev-sales-report-commentary/ https://www.moneytalksnews.com/where-the-u-s-ev-market-stands-a-year-after-trump-ended-tax-credits/]
- LENS career: Track the policy calendar Know which credits, tariffs and rules apply in each market a carmaker sells in. Policy dates drive sales plans.  [src: https://blog.turbotax.intuit.com/tax-reform/electric-vehicle-credits-are-ending-soon-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-what-you-need-to-know-125862/]
- LENS invest: Subsidy-driven demand can vanish US EV sales hit a record before the credit ended, then fell nearly 24% in early 2026. Ask how much demand relies on policy.  [src: https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights-hub/q3-2025-ev-sales-report-commentary/ https://www.moneytalksnews.com/where-the-u-s-ev-market-stands-a-year-after-trump-ended-tax-credits/]
- LENS build_startup: Policy can open or close a market A credit, a tariff or an emission rule can change a market's size within months. Anyone building in EVs should watch all three.  [src: https://blog.turbotax.intuit.com/tax-reform/electric-vehicle-credits-are-ending-soon-under-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-what-you-need-to-know-125862/ https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/united-states-finalizes-section-301-tariff-increases-imports-china]
